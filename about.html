Who We Are

A hardware brand built for the trade

FIXORA serves the North American trade — distributors, lumber yards, builders, and specifiers who need hardware that shows up on time and performs as promised.

We're not a marketplace and not a factory portal. We source factory-direct and back it with a California-based team, so you get manufacturer pricing with a local partner to call.
